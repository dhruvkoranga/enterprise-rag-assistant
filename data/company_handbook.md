# Company Employee Handbook

## Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 24 paid vacation days per calendar year.

Employees should submit vacation requests through the HR portal at least five working days before the planned leave.

Vacation requests longer than five consecutive working days require manager approval.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to team requirements and manager approval.

Employees working remotely must remain available during their team's standard working hours.

## Health Insurance Policy

All full-time employees are eligible for company health insurance after completing 30 days of employment.

The company provides coverage for the employee and eligible dependents according to the current insurance plan.

## Expense Policy

Employees can claim reimbursement for business-related expenses.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date and must include valid receipts.

Expenses above INR 10,000 require prior manager approval.
